Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.88

- The proc glow on cooldown icons can now be coloured, per group, under
  "Proc Glow Colour" beside the Proc Glow tick. It defaults to the gold of
  the game's own glow, so nothing changes until you pick a colour.

  Square icons used to get the game's own glow art, which is a fixed gold
  nothing could recolour. Square now has glow art of this addon's own, drawn
  the same way the round, shield and other shapes already were, so the colour
  applies whatever shape a group uses. Reminder buttons are unchanged.

- "Proc Glow" and "Dim When Unusable" no longer appear on the Buffs and Buff
  Bars groups, where they never did anything - a tracked buff has no proc
  highlight and nothing to dim.
