Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.86

- Essential and Utility icons can now show an ability while it is RUNNING,
  not only while it is on cooldown. Avenging Wrath, Combustion, Metamorphosis
  and the like count down how long is left OF THE BUFF, with their own swipe,
  stay at full colour instead of greying out, and carry a glow deliberately
  different from the proc highlight. The moment the buff ends the real
  cooldown is there underneath, greyed out, exactly as before.

  Off by default. It is "Show While Active", beside Glow On Ready in a
  group's settings, with its own "Active Glow Colour" under it -- the proc
  glow and Glow On Ready use your main glow colour, and this one does not, so
  "the ability is running" and "the ability just lit up" do not look alike.

  It keeps working in combat, which took some doing. A buff's remaining time
  is secret there: it cannot be read, and it cannot be copied from the game's
  own icons either, because the numbers they hold are secret in a way an addon
  is not permitted to pass on. So the active display is not drawn by this
  addon at all -- it is a real aura, handed to the game's own aura engine,
  which draws the icon, the swipe and the countdown itself and shows it only
  while the buff is up. Nothing about the aura ever reaches this addon, which
  is exactly why it cannot break in combat. Tracked buff bars have worked this
  way since V1.76.

  Only a buff that lands on YOU counts. This behaviour existed once and was
  removed in V1.81 because it got Lay on Hands with Empyreal Ward talented
  badly wrong -- a few seconds of sweep, then the icon lighting back up with
  the real cooldown never drawn. Empyreal Ward sits on the person you healed,
  so Lay on Hands keeps showing its cooldown; Avenging Wrath sits on you, so
  it does not.
