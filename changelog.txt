Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.86

- Essential and Utility icons can now show an ability while it is RUNNING,
  not only while it is on cooldown. Avenging Wrath, Combustion, Metamorphosis
  and the like count down how long is left of the buff, stay at full colour
  instead of greying out, and carry a glow deliberately different from the
  proc highlight. The moment the buff ends the real cooldown takes over and
  the icon greys out exactly as before.

  Off by default. It is "Show While Active", beside Glow On Ready in a
  group's settings.

  This existed once and was removed in V1.81, because an icon that draws an
  aura's duration in place of its cooldown gets it badly wrong for something
  like Lay on Hands with Empyreal Ward talented: a few seconds of sweep, then
  the icon lights up again with the real cooldown never drawn at all. What is
  different now is that only a buff landing on YOU counts. Empyreal Ward sits
  on the person you healed, so Lay on Hands keeps showing its cooldown;
  Avenging Wrath sits on you, so it does not.

  In combat this needs the ability to also be in Blizzard's Tracked Buffs --
  once auras are secret, that is the only way left to read how long a running
  one has left. Out of combat it always works.
