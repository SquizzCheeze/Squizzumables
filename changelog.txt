Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.99

- Fixed: "Squizzumables tried to call the protected function" errors
  after a /reload in the new labyrinth, in a Mythic+ key, during a boss or
  in a PvP match, and buff sounds that could all stop until you logged out
  when they were rebuilt there. The game doesn't let addons set up buff
  sounds in those places even out of combat, and only combat was being
  waited out. Squizzumables now waits until you leave or the key, boss or
  match ends; sounds you already had keep playing meanwhile. After a
  /reload inside one, buff sounds come back as soon as it ends.
