Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.92

- Your own feast message can now include {name}, which becomes the name of
  the character placing the feast. Write the message once and it reads
  right on every character. {feast} (the feast's name) already worked and
  is now listed under the message box too.

- A spell can now show in two groups at once. In the CDM settings' Custom
  Icons list, each spell has an "Also in" choice under its group: pick a
  second group and the spell appears there too, with the same cooldown,
  proc glow and range/usable tint as the original. Useful for keeping a big
  cooldown in Essential and also next to your other defensives. The copy
  shows the cooldown only: "Show While Active" stays on the original icon.
  Tracked buffs can't be copied, as they are Blizzard's own icons.
