Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.94

- Fixed: charge counts on Essential, Utility and Custom Icons disappeared
  in combat and could stay missing after you spent a charge. The game hides
  charge counts from addons in combat; they are now shown anyway, the same
  way the game's own Cooldown Manager shows them, and update the moment a
  charge is spent or comes back.
- Text colours and sizes for every Cooldown Manager group (Essential,
  Utility, Buffs, Buff Bars and Custom Icons): the cooldown countdown now
  has its own size and colour, and the charge/stack number its own colour,
  alongside the keybind colour that was already there. A cooldown text
  size of 0 keeps the game's own size, which follows the icon. The
  preview shows them as you change them.
- More for the cursor trail, on the Cursor page:
  - New trail shapes: stars, hearts, diamonds and ducks - the ducks face
    whichever way you move the mouse - or any texture of your own, by game
    atlas name or file path.
  - A colour palette: pick up to six colours and have the trail flow
    through them over time (at a pace you set), or run from your first colour at the head to
    your last at the tail. On its own new Trail Colour tab.
  - Glow or solid blending for any style, the trail's height separately
    from its width to stretch or squash it, and an offset to lay the trail
    a little away from the cursor.
- The options preview for Buffs now shows each icon's border, duration
  countdown and stacks, as the real row draws them, instead of a bare icon.
