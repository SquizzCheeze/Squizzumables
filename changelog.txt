Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.86

- Essential and Utility icons can now show an ability while it is RUNNING,
  not only while it is on cooldown. Avenging Wrath, Combustion, Metamorphosis
  and the like stay at full colour instead of greying out, carry a glow
  deliberately different from the proc highlight, and count down how long is
  left of the buff rather than of the cooldown. The moment the buff ends the
  real cooldown takes over and the icon greys out exactly as before.

  Off by default. It is "Show While Active", beside Glow On Ready in a
  group's settings.

  This is the game's own behaviour for these icons, borrowed rather than
  rebuilt. Blizzard's Cooldown Manager already gives a self buff precedence
  over the spell's cooldown until it is gone; this addon now shows the result
  on its own icons instead of drawing only the cooldown.

  One limit, in combat only: the icon still stays lit and glowing for as long
  as the ability is running, but the number falls back to the spell's
  cooldown. The game hands these particular icons their remaining time as
  plain numbers, and once auras are secret an addon is not allowed to pass
  those on -- unlike the tracked buff bars, which get a form that can be
  handed straight through and so keep their exact countdown in combat.

  Only a buff that lands on YOU counts. This behaviour existed once and was
  removed in V1.81 because it got Lay on Hands with Empyreal Ward talented
  badly wrong -- a few seconds of sweep, then the icon lighting back up with
  the real cooldown never drawn. Empyreal Ward sits on the person you healed,
  so Lay on Hands keeps showing its cooldown; Avenging Wrath sits on you, so
  it does not.
