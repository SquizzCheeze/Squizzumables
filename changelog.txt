Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.74

- Tracked buff bars now have a Cooldown Manager group of their own, drawn as
  bars the way Blizzard draws them. They were previously folded in with the buff
  icons and squeezed into the icon grid, which left the bar too small to read
- The new "Buff Bars" group has its own position, sitting below Buffs by
  default, and its own Bar Width and Bar Height sliders in place of the Icon
  Size and Per Row ones that mean nothing for a bar. Drag it anywhere in unlock
  mode like any other group
- Other addons can anchor to it as Squizzumables_GetCDMGroupFrame("Buff Bars")
- Fixed Cooldown Manager groups with nothing currently active having no green
  drag zone in unlock mode, so there was no way to position them. The group hid
  itself again a fraction of a second after unlock mode revealed it. Most
  visible on the new Buff Bars group, since tracked bars are procs and none of
  them are up while you are out of combat arranging your UI -- the giveaway was
  that the zone appeared if Blizzard's Edit Mode happened to be open too
- Fixed dragging a Cooldown Manager group saving the wrong position, so it
  jumped somewhere else the next time anything rebuilt it. The drag was saving
  reliably -- it was saving a number that meant something different to the one
  reading it back, because the position was read relative to whatever edge the
  frame had ended up anchored to and then restored as an offset from the centre
  of the screen. It is now measured from the centre either way. Groups anchored
  to another group were never affected, which is why this went unnoticed
