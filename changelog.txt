Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.92

- Placeholders in your own messages. The feast message and callouts can
  now include {name}, which becomes the character sending it, and {zone},
  the dungeon or zone you are in - write a message once and it reads right
  on every character. The feast message also takes {feast} (the feast's
  name), which already worked and is now listed under the message box.
  Raid-marker codes such as {skull} still work as before.

- A spell can now show in two groups at once. In the CDM settings' Custom
  Icons list, each spell has an "Also in" choice under its group: pick a
  second group and the spell appears there too, with the same cooldown,
  proc glow and range/usable tint as the original. Useful for keeping a big
  cooldown in Essential and also next to your other defensives. The copy
  shows the cooldown only: "Show While Active" stays on the original icon.
  Tracked buffs can't be copied, as they are Blizzard's own icons.

- The bag reminder also warns when you are running low, not only when you
  have run out: "LOW ON FLASK (1 LEFT)". By default it warns on your last
  one; set how many under Text Reminders > Bags ("Warn when running low",
  0 turns it off). Several of one kind, such as two flask types, count
  together. Having none is still shown first.
