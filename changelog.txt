Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.82

- Fixed a tracked buff bar drawn in Blizzard's style instead of this addon's
  whenever the buff comes from a ground effect. In a row of bars, Consecration
  looked nothing like the bars beside it: Blizzard's own rounded outline and
  fill rather than yours, with no border and no spacing of its own.

  A ground effect puts no buff on you -- the game tracks it as a totem instead
  -- so there was no aura for this addon to attach its own bar to, and the slot
  was handed back to Blizzard's frame every pass. Those bars now mirror
  Blizzard's own fill and countdown onto a bar of ours, so a ground effect
  reads exactly like every other tracked buff: your bar texture, your colour,
  your font, your border. Death Knight Death and Decay and anything else driven
  the same way are fixed by the same change

- Tracked buff bar placeholders draw their border. The "Always Show Buffs"
  slots were drawing without one, so a row mixing held slots and live buffs
  showed two different looks
