Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.1

- Fixed: a group buff button (Skyfury, Arcane Intellect, Battle Shout and
  the rest) could get stuck on screen after you had the buff, together with
  an "Unknown macro option: buff" error, until a /reload. The button was
  trying to hide itself in combat with a rule the game does not support. It
  now just goes away on the next refresh out of combat.
- Class buff buttons now show during a Mythic+ key, out of combat:
  Arcane Intellect, Skyfury, Earth Shield, Water and Lightning Shield,
  poisons, paladin auras and Rites, and the rest. They used to be hidden
  for the whole key. Inside a key the group buffs hide in combat like the
  other buttons, where elsewhere they stay up so you can rebuff someone.
- The theme is now shared with SquizzFrames: picking a preset in either
  addon changes both. It is saved for your whole account rather than per
  profile, so switching profiles no longer changes it. Your current choice
  carries over.
