Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.61

- Fixed the per-item "Min" minutes box not saving. It only committed when you pressed Enter, so
  typing a number and clicking away threw it out and the box redrew at the stored value -- which
  looked like the setting reverting to 30 on its own. It now saves when you click away, tab off
  or close the panel, the same as every other box in the addon. Affected every Min box (food,
  flask, oil, augment rune, custom items, self-buff spells and the Coach's Whistle), not just
  food. Escape still discards, and clearing the box and clicking away now leaves the value alone
  rather than reading as 0
- The "Min" boxes now take the mouse wheel: click into one and scroll to nudge it a minute at a
  time, or hold Shift for five. Only while the box has focus, so scrolling the item list with the
  cursor over a box still scrolls the list instead of quietly rewriting a threshold
- Every text box in the settings panel is now drawn in the addon's own style -- square 1px
  borders matching the checkboxes, buttons and sliders, instead of Blizzard's rounded gold search
  border. They light up in the accent colour when focused, and preview it on hover
