Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.87

- Trinkets in Essential and Utility show their cooldown again: the swipe, the
  countdown, and greying out while on cooldown. Some trinkets were being
  treated as a spell, and asking about the spell always said "ready".

- "Show While Active" now works for trinkets: while a trinket's buff is up
  its icon shows the buff, glows and stays in colour, like an ability does.

- "Show While Active" is no longer offered on the Buffs and Buff Bars groups,
  where it never did anything. Turn it on in Essential or Utility (or a custom
  group) instead.
