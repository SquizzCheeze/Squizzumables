Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.6

- New: two more bundled sounds, "Nori" and "SQUIZZ! From Upsidedownland".

V2.5

- New (WoW Forever): Hunter aspects are class buffs. While no aspect is up,
  each one you know gets a button: Hawk, Monkey, Wild, Beast, Cheetah and
  Pack. Untick any you don't want on the Class Buffs tab.
- Fixed (WoW Forever): the No Pet reminder no longer shows before you have
  learned Call Pet at level 10.
- Fixed: the lust song could replay after a /reload or a loading screen
  (porting to a city) while you still had Sated or Exhaustion.

V2.4

- New: three bundled sounds, "The Ducky of the Opera", "For Ducky, Every
  Song Is Sung" and "He Started It".

V2.3

- New: Squizzumables now runs on WoW Forever as well as retail. Forever has
  its own food, flask, weapon oil and class buff lists, with every rank of a
  spell counted. Buttons cast your highest rank and stay hidden until you
  have learned the spell. Rogue poisons appear with the weapon oils.
- Not on Forever yet: elixirs, Paladin blessings and augment runes.

V2.2

- New: Rotation Helper (Cooldowns > Rotation Helper, off until you switch
  it on). Shows the spell Blizzard's rotation helper suggests next on an
  icon you can put anywhere with Edit Mode, with the cooldown swipe, red
  when out of range, grey when it can't be cast (blue when you're short on
  resources) and a glow on procs. It is Blizzard's suggestion - one spell
  at a time - shown where you want it.
- New: a Ready Now row beside the Rotation Helper icon (its own tab on the
  same page). List the spells you want, per spec and in your order, or
  fill it from the rotation helper's spells that have cooldowns. Each icon
  is bright when the spell is ready and fades while it is on cooldown
  (how far is up to you, down to hidden), with the same swipe, range,
  resource and proc cues as the main icon.
- The hunter NO PET reminder now also shows when your pet is DEAD (as
  PET DEAD, so you know to Revive it rather than Call it), including in the
  middle of a fight - before, a dead pet still counted as "having a pet".
- Fixed: text reminders (NO PET, NO FOOD IN BAGS, BEACON and the rest) drew
  on top of Blizzard's windows, such as the world map and the Great Vault.
  They now sit behind them.
- Rotation Helper icons show their keybind (from your action bars, the same
  as the Cooldown Manager groups; size, position and colour on the Main
  Icon tab), and can show cooldown numbers.
- New: Low Health Glow for Cooldown Manager and custom groups (Behaviour
  tab). Tick the icons in a group that should glow while your health is
  below a threshold you set (default 35%) and the ability is ready, in a
  colour of your own - a nudge to use a defensive or a potion. Works in
  combat.
- Fixed: potions and Healthstones you added to Blizzard's Cooldown Manager
  showed on Blizzard's bars but never on ours. Combat Potion, Health
  Potion, Healthstone and Demonic Healthstone now show with their
  cooldowns, read from whichever one you carry, with that item's own icon.
  An icon only shows while you have one in your bags.
