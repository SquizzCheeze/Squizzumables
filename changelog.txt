Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.62

- Cooldown Manager sound alerts now follow spec and talent changes without a reload. Two things
  were wrong. Talent swaps were not being noticed at all -- the module only watched
  PLAYER_SPECIALIZATION_CHANGED, which does not fire when you change talents inside the same
  spec, so it now also watches TRAIT_CONFIG_UPDATED and PLAYER_PVP_TALENT_UPDATE, the same
  events Blizzard's own Cooldown Manager uses, plus its "cooldown data changed" callback
- And the addon's memory of which spell belongs to which cooldown was never cleared. Those
  lookups are a cache over Blizzard's viewer frames, and the entries for the spec you left stayed
  behind pointing at cooldowns that had since been recycled onto other spells -- so an alert
  could end up reading the wrong spell's state. They are now emptied and rebuilt whenever the
  cooldown set changes
- The Cooldowns settings tab now rebuilds on a spec or talent change too. Groups, assignments and
  the sound alert list are all per-spec, so leaving the panel open across a spec change showed
  the previous spec's setup until something else happened to refresh it
- Kelerts can now fire in combat. Since 12.1 the game hides almost every aura from addons while
  you are in combat -- which is exactly when your alert was supposed to go off -- so an alert on,
  say, a healing cooldown worked in a city and stayed silent in the pull you made it for. The
  addon now hands the spell and the sound to the game up front and lets it play the sound itself,
  so there is nothing left for it to hide. Alerts with a single spell, one sound and no sound
  loop switch to this automatically; nothing to turn on
- The catch, and it is worth knowing: the game plays the sound and tells the addon nothing, so an
  alert delivered this way cannot show its image in combat. The picture still needs an aura the
  addon can see. Sound is the part that now always works
- The lust alert is unchanged -- image and sound both. It watches five debuffs at once, and those
  are among the few the game still lets addons see, so it never had this problem
- The Kelerts tab is now two things. The lust alert stays exactly as it was, image and all. Below
  it is Buff Sounds: a grid of your spec's own buffs, built from your spellbook, so it follows
  every talent and spec change on its own with nothing to keep updated. Click one to give it a
  sound when it lands, when it drops, or both
- Your existing custom Kelerts have moved into that grid, keeping their sounds. Their images are
  gone, because there is no way to keep them -- the game plays these sounds itself and never tells
  the addon it did, which is the same reason they now work in combat at all
- Buffs the grid does not list can still be added by spell ID. Worth knowing for the handful of
  spells whose cast and buff have different IDs
