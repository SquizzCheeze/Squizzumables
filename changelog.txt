Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.93

- Mouse cursor rings, on a new Cursor page in the options. A dot at the
  cursor's tip, a ring around it, your global cooldown filling a ring
  inside it and your cast or channel filling one outside it - in your
  class colour, a colour you pick, or a cycling rainbow. Size, opacity,
  each ring on or off, and Only In Combat. The rings hide while you turn
  the camera with a mouse button held, since the cursor itself is hidden
  then. Off by default; turn it on under Cursor.
- An optional trail behind the cursor as you move it, fading out over a
  length you choose. Rainbow by default, running through the colours
  along the trail, or class or custom colour, with its own size and a
  rainbow speed shared with the rings.
