Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.91

- The lust alert no longer plays again for a lust that happened earlier. It
  could replay while you still had Sated or Exhaustion from a key you had
  already finished: after leaving a dungeon, after a /reload, or when changing
  zones. It now plays only when the debuff has just been applied.

- The "Loop sound" option now warns that it is for short sounds only. Looping
  a long track, such as the 40 second bundled lust tracks, stacks copies of it
  on top of each other, and in a busy fight the game can then start cutting
  sounds off - the alert's included.
