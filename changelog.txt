Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.81

- Fixed a spell that a talent replaces never showing its cooldown on the
  Cooldown Manager. It stayed lit and showed no countdown text while the
  spellbook clearly had it on cooldown.

  Some talents swap a spell for another version of itself, and the cooldown
  then belongs to the replacement. Lay on Hands with Empyreal Ward is one:
  the game reports it as a different spell entirely, and the original answers
  "ready, no cooldown" forever. Every cooldown, charge, usability and sorting
  check now follows the replacement, so a talented spell reads exactly like an
  untalented one. Spells that no talent replaces are unaffected

- Fixed a tracked cooldown briefly showing a buff's duration instead of its
  own cooldown. If the spell also applied a buff, the icon swept for the few
  seconds of that buff and then lit back up, never showing the real cooldown
  or its text. Cooldown icons now always show their cooldown; tracking the
  buff itself is what the Buffs and Buff Bars groups are for
