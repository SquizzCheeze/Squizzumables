Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.2

- New: Rotation Helper (Cooldowns > Rotation Helper, off until you switch
  it on). Shows the spell Blizzard's rotation helper suggests next on an
  icon you can put anywhere with Edit Mode, with the cooldown swipe, red
  when out of range, grey when it can't be cast (blue when you're short on
  resources) and a glow on procs. It is Blizzard's suggestion - one spell
  at a time - shown where you want it.
- New: a Ready Now row beside the Rotation Helper icon (its own tab on the
  same page). List the spells you want, per spec and in your order, or
  fill it from the rotation helper's spells that have cooldowns. Each icon
  is bright when the spell is ready and fades while it is on cooldown
  (how far is up to you, down to hidden), with the same swipe, range,
  resource and proc cues as the main icon.
- The hunter NO PET reminder now also shows when your pet is DEAD (as
  PET DEAD, so you know to Revive it rather than Call it), including in the
  middle of a fight - before, a dead pet still counted as "having a pet".
- Fixed: text reminders (NO PET, NO FOOD IN BAGS, BEACON and the rest) drew
  on top of Blizzard's windows, such as the world map and the Great Vault.
  They now sit behind them.
- Rotation Helper icons show their keybind (from your action bars, the same
  as the Cooldown Manager groups; size, position and colour on the Main
  Icon tab), and can show cooldown numbers.
