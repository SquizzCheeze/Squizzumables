Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.93

- Mouse cursor rings, on a new Cursor page in the options. A dot at the
  cursor's tip, a ring around it, your global cooldown filling a ring
  inside it and your cast or channel filling one outside it - in your
  class colour, a colour you pick, or a cycling rainbow. Size, opacity,
  each ring on or off, and Only In Combat. The rings stay put while you
  hold a mouse button to turn the camera (or tick Hide While Turning The
  Camera). Off by default; turn it on under Cursor.
- An optional trail behind the cursor as you move it. Rainbow by default,
  running through the colours along the trail, or class or custom colour.
  Choose its style (soft glow, solid dots or rings), how long it lingers
  (up to 3 seconds), its opacity, how much it shrinks as it fades, how
  dense it is and how wide. The Sparkle style drifts, falls and twinkles
  like sparks off a sparkler.
- Click Burst: a burst of particles flies out from the cursor when you
  left-click, round the colour wheel with a rainbow colour.
- The ring can warn you on low health, turning amber and then red as your
  health drops, over whatever colour it already is. Works in combat. Choose
  the health it starts at.
- A different ring colour while you are in combat, if you want one.
- Shake To Find Cursor: shake the mouse left and right and a big ring
  shrinks onto the cursor, to find it in a busy fight. On by default once
  the cursor is turned on.
