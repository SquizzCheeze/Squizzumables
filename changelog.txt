Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.95

- Border Inside Icon, a new option under each Cooldown Manager group's
  Appearance: the border is drawn over the icon's own edge instead of
  around the outside, for square and shaped icons, buffs and buff bars.
  The group then takes up exactly its icons' size, so a SquizzFrames cast
  bar or resource bar set to match its width now lines up with the outer
  edge of the border instead of falling short by the border's width. Off
  by default, so nothing changes until you tick it.
