Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.0

- CDM Alerts: Buff Alerts and the CDM Sounds page are now one tab
  (Kelerts > CDM Alerts). Your buffs and your Essential and Utility
  cooldowns sit in one grid, and clicking any of them opens the same
  editor. Cooldowns can now show an image as well as play a sound - the
  same images as buffs (screen edge glow, flames, the ( ) arcs and the
  rest, or your own) - on Ready or On cooldown, for a few seconds or
  until the spell's next event (a Ready image stays up until you use the
  spell). It works in combat. The CDM Sounds sidebar page is gone.
- Buff sounds you had on the old CDM Sounds page (Active, On Aura Applied,
  On Aura Removed) are moved to that buff in CDM Alerts automatically,
  without replacing a sound you had already set there.
- /squizz cdms now opens CDM Alerts.
- The options window's sidebar is grouped: Reminders (Consumables, Class
  Buffs, Text Reminders), Cooldowns (Cooldown Manager, Custom Icons),
  Alerts (Kelerts, Sounds), Group (Raid Tools, Callouts), Fun (Cursor) and
  General (Settings). Every page is the same as before; three are renamed
  to say what they hold (Items is Consumables, Reminders is Text
  Reminders, CDM is Cooldown Manager).
- Themes: Settings opens with a Theme section, in place of "Use class colour for
  headings and accents", with five looks - Squizz Gold (the default),
  Dark Glass (see-through panels, thin light borders), Class Colour,
  Rainbow (an accent that slowly cycles through the colours) and Minimal
  (no panels behind the frames, just icons and text). The accent changes
  at once; Dark Glass and Minimal offer a reload for the panels and
  borders. Your old choice carries over: class colour on stays Class
  Colour. Switching profile now brings that profile's theme with it.
- Preview column: Consumables, Class Buffs and Settings show your reminder
  buttons on the right of the page - a missing flask, food running low
  and your class buff - and they change as you edit: size, spacing, grow
  direction, labels, fonts, icon shape, glow and theme. They are drawn by
  the same code as the real buttons. The options window is a little wider
  (900) to make room.
- Cursor > Trail: the Test Shark button's bite now shows above the
  settings window instead of underneath it. Real bites still follow your
  Frame Strata setting.
- Colours now come from one theme, the groundwork for the 2.0 look. The
  only change you can see: the headings on the Cooldown Manager pages used
  their own slightly different gold and ignored "Use class colour for
  headings and accents"; they now match the rest of the window.
- New installs and new profiles start with the warm gold accent; class
  colour stays one tick away. Existing profiles keep what they had.
- The reminder buttons now grow the way you set them without a second
  setting: Grow Down keeps the top button where you placed it, Grow Up the
  bottom one, Right the left end, Left the right end, Outward the middle.
  The separate Anchor Point setting is gone. A vertical stack set to grow
  down used to grow up and down from its middle unless Anchor Point was
  changed to Top as well.
- Fixed: after a /reload inside a delve, buff sounds stayed silent until
  you left it. They were waiting out the delve's addon restriction, which
  turns out not to block them; they now set themselves up straight away.
- Fixed: every reminder button could appear at once, out of combat, when
  the game had stopped letting addons see your buffs (seen in the 12.1.5
  labyrinth after a /reload; only logging out cleared it). The buttons now
  keep showing what they last knew until your buffs can be read again,
  the same as they already did in combat.
