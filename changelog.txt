Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.8

- Fixed (WoW Forever): Mark of the Wild, Power Word: Fortitude and Arcane
  Intellect are single-target there, so the button cast on whoever you had
  targeted (a friendly NPC took the buff and your mana). The button now
  buffs the next person missing it, you first, then tanks, healers and the
  rest, with their name under it and a count of how many are missing.
  Right-click it to pick someone else: a grid of up to 8 names opens, the
  last tile showing "+N" when more are missing. It closes when the mouse
  leaves. The tooltip shows your highest rank instead of rank 1.
- New: UI Scale (Settings, under Theme), for every character on the
  account. It can go below the game's own 0.64 limit, with Small, Medium,
  Large and Pixel Perfect presets. While ElvUI is loaded, ElvUI keeps
  setting the scale and Squizzumables copies its value, so turning ElvUI
  off later leaves every frame where it was.
- New: Data Texts (Interface > Data Texts), off until you switch them on.
  A strip of live readouts under the minimap, or anywhere you place it in
  Edit Mode, holding up to three texts. Guild shows how many members are
  online; hover for the list (level, name, status, zone; Shift for ranks
  and notes), click for the guild window, right-click to invite or
  whisper. Friends does the same for character and Battle.net friends,
  grouped by game, and its right-click menu also sets your status and your
  Battle.net broadcast. If you use ElvUI, its font, colours and minimap
  texts are copied over the first time, so the two look the same.
- New: Item Bars (Interface > Item Bars), off until you switch them on. Up
  to five bars of item buttons that fill themselves from your bags, quest
  log and gear. Each bar shows the groups you tick: quest items, gear with
  a Use:, containers, holiday and other usable items, potions, flasks,
  vantus runes, food, augment runes, weapon oils, bandages and a list of
  your own. Groups are worked out from the items themselves, so a new
  expansion's potions need no update. Ctrl-right-click a button to hide
  that item. If you use WindTools' Extra Items Bar, its bars' positions,
  sizes and groups are copied over the first time.
- New: Action Bars (Interface > Action Bars), off until you switch them
  on. Up to 13 bars, numbered as ElvUI numbers them so each shows the
  same action slots, with your existing keybinds. Bar 1 pages for
  vehicles, forms, stealth and Shift-number like the main bar. Per bar:
  buttons, rows, size, spacing, growth, background, empty buttons,
  click-through, opacity, mouseover fade and a visibility condition.
  Square icons, thin borders, proc glows, cooldown numbers and a Leave
  Vehicle button. They stay off while ElvUI's own action bars are on;
  your ElvUI bars' positions and settings are copied over the first
  time. Keybinds show short: S-1, A-C-2, MWU, MWD, M4, N5.
  Built on LibActionButton-1.0 (BSD licence, included).
- Action Bars also take over the stance bar, pet bar and micro menu
  (their own tabs): buttons per row, size, spacing, growth, background,
  opacity, mouseover fade and a visibility condition, placed in Edit Mode.
  The buttons are Blizzard's own, so their keybinds and pet autocast work
  as before. Your ElvUI settings for these are copied over the first time.
- Action Bars: Hide Blizzard's Bag Bar (General tab, on by default).
  ElvUI hides the bag bar along with its own action bars, so it came
  back once those were turned off.
- New: Chat (Interface > Chat), off until you switch it on. Two chat
  panels you place in Edit Mode, with sizes, backgrounds and a tab strip;
  the main chat window sits in the left one. Blizzard's chat borders,
  buttons and scroll bar are cleared; chat and tab fonts, the selected
  tab's colour, where the edit box sits, fading, lines kept, jumping back
  to the newest line and timestamps (your local time). It stays off while
  ElvUI's chat is on; your ElvUI chat settings are copied over the first
  time. More of ElvUI's chat options follow.
- Chat: a strip of three data texts under each chat panel (Chat > Data
  Texts tab), as in ElvUI, copied from your ElvUI chat data texts.
- Chat: Messages and Edit Box tabs. Clickable web links (click to copy),
  item and spell tooltips on hover, short channel names ([G], [P], [2]),
  a timestamp colour, names of people you know in their class colour,
  role icons for your group, keywords highlighted with a sound, alert
  sounds per channel, hiding repeated spam in Say, Yell and channels,
  arrow keys without Alt, typed lines remembered across reloads, a
  character count, lines per mouse wheel turn and a Copy Chat button.
  Your ElvUI choices are copied over the first time. Messages hidden from
  add-ons during encounters show exactly as Blizzard draws them.
- Chat: Blizzard's chat menu, channels and voice mute/deafen buttons in a
  row at the top right of the left panel (right-clicking Copy Chat also
  opens the chat menu); a small arrow on each data text strip hides or
  shows that panel; and Keep Chat History (Edit Box tab, off until you
  switch it on), which brings each window's recent chat back after a
  reload or your next login on that character.
- Chat: the chat text no longer runs out past the right of its panel
  when the panel sits near the left edge of the screen.
- Data Texts: six new texts, usable on the minimap strip as well.
  Talent/Loot Specialization (right-click to switch either), Durability,
  Currencies (the ones you track), System (frame rate, latency, add-on
  memory), Time (local and realm, with your lockouts) and Gold (this
  session, and every character on the account).
- New: Tweaks (Interface > Tweaks), each off until you switch it on:
  auto repair (guild funds first, or your own gold), the talking head's
  size or hiding it, Blizzard's cooldown numbers on or off, collapsing the
  objective tracker in Mythic+, a totem bar (Blizzard's own totem buttons,
  so tooltips and right-click to cancel still work) and a reputation bar
  for the faction you watch (renown, friendship and paragon included). The
  two bars are placed in Edit Mode. Each waits while ElvUI does the same
  job, and your ElvUI settings for them are copied over the first time.
- New: Buffs and Debuffs (Interface > Buffs and Debuffs), off until you
  switch it on. Your buffs and debuffs in two frames of their own, placed
  in Edit Mode: icon size, growth direction, icons per row, rows, spacing,
  sorting, a cooldown swipe, and the timer's and stacks' position and font.
  Square icons with a thin edge in the debuff type's colour, weapon
  enchants before your buffs, right-click to cancel a buff, and boss
  debuffs hidden from add-ons shown too. Blizzard's own buff and debuff
  frames are hidden (untick to keep them). It waits while ElvUI's Buffs and
  Debuffs are on; your ElvUI layout and positions are copied over the
  first time.
- New: Tooltip (Interface > Tooltip), off until you switch it on. The
  game's tooltip with a flat dark look and a thin edge, your fonts for the
  text, title line and comparisons, and where it shows: above the right
  chat panel, a spot you place in Edit Mode, or at the cursor. It can hide
  in combat (or show only with Shift, Ctrl or Alt). A health bar above or
  below with percent or current / max. Players get their class colour,
  title, guild and rank, realm on Shift, AFK/DND, level in its difficulty
  colour and a coloured spec line; creatures their reaction colour, level,
  elite or rare tag and type. Both show their role and their target, and
  the PvP and faction lines are dropped. It waits while ElvUI's tooltip is
  on; your ElvUI tooltip settings are copied over the first time.
- Tooltip: an Extras tab. Hold Shift over a player for their item level;
  their Mythic+ score and best run, and the mount they ride (Ctrl for where
  it comes from), all out of combat. IDs for spells, items, auras,
  creatures, currencies, toys, mounts and quests (always, never, or with a
  key held). On items: how many you have in your bags and bank, the stack
  size, an edge in the item's quality colour, and the sell price as
  Blizzard's coins, a line of text or not at all.
- Tooltip: a More tab, after WindTools' tooltip extras. Race and spec
  icons on players, an icon by the name of items, spells, toys, mounts,
  currencies and achievements, a faction icon, and a battle pet's type
  icon and ID. The Mythic+ best run shows its dungeon and a + for each
  upgrade it was timed by; keystones show yours, and other players' when
  they run an addon that shares them. Hold Shift for raid progression in
  this season's raids; tier set pieces sit beside the item level; quest
  creatures show how much each kill counts. On group listings, the members
  by role, class and spec. The health bar and its text can be moved up or
  down. Your WindTools tooltip settings are copied over the first time.
  The quest data is MMOSimca's, from LibObjectiveProgress, used with
  permission given in its notes.
- New: Minimap (Interface > Minimap), off until you switch it on. A square
  (or round) minimap you size, scale and place in Edit Mode, with a
  border of the size and colour you choose (or your class colour).
  Blizzard's header, zoom buttons and compass are cleared; the zone
  name sits on top (always, on mouseover or never) in its PvP colour. The
  icons round it - tracking, mail, crafting orders, instance difficulty,
  the expansion summary, calendar, event link and the add-on list - can
  each be placed in a corner, sized, nudged or hidden. Right-click for
  tracking, middle-click or Shift-right-click for a menu of the game's
  windows, and the zoom can reset itself after a few seconds. Blizzard's
  clock can show under it. It waits while ElvUI's minimap is on; your
  ElvUI minimap settings and position are copied over the first time.
- New: World Map (Interface > World Map), off until you switch it on. The
  map and quest log window's scale, fading while you move (how far and how
  fast; never while the mouse is over the map), no dark backdrop behind the
  full-size map, and Blizzard's coordinates for you and the cursor. The
  parts of each zone you haven't explored are shown too (tinted if you
  like), on retail and WoW Forever, from the game's own map data. It
  waits while ElvUI's or WindTools' world map is on; their settings are
  copied over the first time.
- New: Map Pins (Interface > Map Pins), off until you switch it on. A pin
  you place is tracked straight away, middle-click a pin to remove it, the
  arrow can show at any distance (Blizzard hides it past 1,000 yards), and
  the distance under it takes your font, size and colour, as a plain
  number if you like. /way also takes coordinates as guide sites write
  them: "/way 45.2 67.1" for the zone you're in, "/way #2248 45.2, 67.1"
  for another (not while TomTom is loaded). It waits while WindTools'
  Super Tracker is on; its settings are copied over the first time.
- New: Objective Tracker (Interface > Objective Tracker), off until you
  switch it on. Restyles Blizzard's quest tracker: fonts for the section
  headers and the quest text, colours (or class colour) for headers, quest
  titles and objectives with their mouse-over colours, a solid, gradient or
  class-coloured bar under each section header, no dash before objectives,
  and "3/10" coloured red to green as it fills, with the percentage if you
  like. Works on retail and WoW Forever. It waits while WindTools' Objective
  Tracker is on; your WindTools settings are copied over the first time.
  Switching it off puts Blizzard's look back after a /reload.
- New: World Events (Interface > World Events), off until you switch it
  on. A strip under the minimap (or anywhere, in Edit Mode) of the events
  going on now and coming up next: icon, name, time left or time until it
  starts over a thin bar, and a tick once you have its rewards. Hover for
  the game's own tooltip, click to open the map at it, right-click for the
  game's reminder when it starts. It reads the game's own event list (the
  map's Events tab), so new events appear by themselves. It waits while
  WindTools' Event Tracker is on; its size and font are copied over the
  first time.
- Minimap: the group finder eye is one of its icons now (bottom right,
  small, as in ElvUI), placed, sized or hidden like the others, instead of
  sitting by the micro menu.
- Chat: Up and Down in the edit box go through what you typed without
  holding Alt (Alt+Up for /cast and other secure commands).
- Squizzumables now ships CallbackHandler-1.0 itself. Its fonts list had
  been relying on another addon to load it.

V2.7

- New: four more bundled sounds, "Defensives", "Herding Cats",
  "Herding Cats 2" and "Kelli Flailing".

V2.6

- New: two more bundled sounds, "Nori" and "SQUIZZ! From Upsidedownland".

V2.5

- New (WoW Forever): Hunter aspects are class buffs. While no aspect is up,
  each one you know gets a button: Hawk, Monkey, Wild, Beast, Cheetah and
  Pack. Untick any you don't want on the Class Buffs tab.
- Fixed (WoW Forever): the No Pet reminder no longer shows before you have
  learned Call Pet at level 10.
- Fixed: the lust song could replay after a /reload or a loading screen
  (porting to a city) while you still had Sated or Exhaustion.

V2.4

- New: three bundled sounds, "The Ducky of the Opera", "For Ducky, Every
  Song Is Sung" and "He Started It".

V2.3

- New: Squizzumables now runs on WoW Forever as well as retail. Forever has
  its own food, flask, weapon oil and class buff lists, with every rank of a
  spell counted. Buttons cast your highest rank and stay hidden until you
  have learned the spell. Rogue poisons appear with the weapon oils.
- Not on Forever yet: elixirs, Paladin blessings and augment runes.

V2.2

- New: Rotation Helper (Cooldowns > Rotation Helper, off until you switch
  it on). Shows the spell Blizzard's rotation helper suggests next on an
  icon you can put anywhere with Edit Mode, with the cooldown swipe, red
  when out of range, grey when it can't be cast (blue when you're short on
  resources) and a glow on procs. It is Blizzard's suggestion - one spell
  at a time - shown where you want it.
- New: a Ready Now row beside the Rotation Helper icon (its own tab on the
  same page). List the spells you want, per spec and in your order, or
  fill it from the rotation helper's spells that have cooldowns. Each icon
  is bright when the spell is ready and fades while it is on cooldown
  (how far is up to you, down to hidden), with the same swipe, range,
  resource and proc cues as the main icon.
- The hunter NO PET reminder now also shows when your pet is DEAD (as
  PET DEAD, so you know to Revive it rather than Call it), including in the
  middle of a fight - before, a dead pet still counted as "having a pet".
- Fixed: text reminders (NO PET, NO FOOD IN BAGS, BEACON and the rest) drew
  on top of Blizzard's windows, such as the world map and the Great Vault.
  They now sit behind them.
- Rotation Helper icons show their keybind (from your action bars, the same
  as the Cooldown Manager groups; size, position and colour on the Main
  Icon tab), and can show cooldown numbers.
- New: Low Health Glow for Cooldown Manager and custom groups (Behaviour
  tab). Tick the icons in a group that should glow while your health is
  below a threshold you set (default 35%) and the ability is ready, in a
  colour of your own - a nudge to use a defensive or a potion. Works in
  combat.
- Fixed: potions and Healthstones you added to Blizzard's Cooldown Manager
  showed on Blizzard's bars but never on ours. Combat Potion, Health
  Potion, Healthstone and Demonic Healthstone now show with their
  cooldowns, read from whichever one you carry, with that item's own icon.
  An icon only shows while you have one in your bags.
