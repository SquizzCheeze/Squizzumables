Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.96

- Mouse cursor: the rings no longer end up slightly off the cursor after
  turning the camera with the right mouse button. The hidden cursor drifts
  a little while you turn, and the rings now follow it.
- Buff images can build up with stacks: Reveal by Stacks uncovers the image
  a piece at a time as a buff stacks, whole at the number you choose, left
  to right or bottom to top. Set the ( ) arcs to 2 for a two-stack buff and
  1 stack shows the left arc, 2 stacks both. The game itself tracks the
  stacks, so it works in combat.
- Hide all of Blizzard's spell alerts (Buff Alerts, off by default): hides
  the proc art Blizzard draws round the middle of the screen for every
  spell, not just the buffs you give an image. The alert sound still plays.
- Fixed: a buff image picked from Buff Alerts could never show when the
  buff's icon there is its talent rather than the buff itself (Infusion of
  Light, Divine Purpose). The real buff IDs were learned from the Cooldown
  Manager only after the image had been built, and the image was never
  rebuilt. It now rebuilds as soon as they are learned, after login and on
  every talent or spec change. A Blizzard spell alert for a buff with an
  image is also matched by name, which teaches the image that ID too.
- Fixed: Blizzard's spell alert art for a buff with an image could still
  show for about a second on the first proc after logging in or reloading.
  The buff's other spell IDs are now remembered between sessions.
