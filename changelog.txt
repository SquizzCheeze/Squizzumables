Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.64

- Fixed buff sounds silently switching themselves off when you opened the settings with a slash
  command. Registering a buff sound is a protected call, and the game blocks those when the chain
  starts at the chat box -- so typing /sq config cleared every registration and then could not put
  them back, leaving you with no buff sounds until your next login. Opening the panel any other
  way was unaffected, which is why it took a bug report to find. The registration now happens a
  moment later, out of that chain, where the game allows it
