Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.8

- Fixed (WoW Forever): Mark of the Wild, Power Word: Fortitude and Arcane
  Intellect are single-target there, so the button cast on whoever you had
  targeted (a friendly NPC took the buff and your mana). The button now
  buffs the next person missing it, you first, then tanks, healers and the
  rest, with their name under it and a count of how many are missing.
  Right-click it to pick someone else: a grid of up to 8 names opens, the
  last tile showing "+N" when more are missing. It closes when the mouse
  leaves. The tooltip shows your highest rank instead of rank 1.
- New: UI Scale (Settings, under Theme), for every character on the
  account. It can go below the game's own 0.64 limit, with Small, Medium,
  Large and Pixel Perfect presets. While ElvUI is loaded, ElvUI keeps
  setting the scale and Squizzumables copies its value, so turning ElvUI
  off later leaves every frame where it was.
- New: Data Texts (Interface > Data Texts), off until you switch them on.
  A strip of live readouts under the minimap, or anywhere you place it in
  Edit Mode, holding up to three texts. Guild shows how many members are
  online; hover for the list (level, name, status, zone; Shift for ranks
  and notes), click for the guild window, right-click to invite or
  whisper. Friends does the same for character and Battle.net friends,
  grouped by game, and its right-click menu also sets your status and your
  Battle.net broadcast. If you use ElvUI, its font, colours and minimap
  texts are copied over the first time, so the two look the same.
- New: Item Bars (Interface > Item Bars), off until you switch them on. Up
  to five bars of item buttons that fill themselves from your bags, quest
  log and gear. Each bar shows the groups you tick: quest items, gear with
  a Use:, containers, holiday and other usable items, potions, flasks,
  vantus runes, food, augment runes, weapon oils, bandages and a list of
  your own. Groups are worked out from the items themselves, so a new
  expansion's potions need no update. Ctrl-right-click a button to hide
  that item. If you use WindTools' Extra Items Bar, its bars' positions,
  sizes and groups are copied over the first time.
- New: Action Bars (Interface > Action Bars), off until you switch them
  on. Up to 13 bars, numbered as ElvUI numbers them so each shows the
  same action slots, with your existing keybinds. Bar 1 pages for
  vehicles, forms, stealth and Shift-number like the main bar. Per bar:
  buttons, rows, size, spacing, growth, background, empty buttons,
  click-through, opacity, mouseover fade and a visibility condition.
  Square icons, thin borders, proc glows, cooldown numbers and a Leave
  Vehicle button. They stay off while ElvUI's own action bars are on;
  your ElvUI bars' positions and settings are copied over the first
  time. Keybinds show short: S-1, A-C-2, MWU, MWD, M4, N5.
  Built on LibActionButton-1.0 (BSD licence, included).
- Action Bars also take over the stance bar, pet bar and micro menu
  (their own tabs): buttons per row, size, spacing, growth, background,
  opacity, mouseover fade and a visibility condition, placed in Edit Mode.
  The buttons are Blizzard's own, so their keybinds and pet autocast work
  as before. Your ElvUI settings for these are copied over the first time.
- Action Bars: Hide Blizzard's Bag Bar (General tab, on by default).
  ElvUI hides the bag bar along with its own action bars, so it came
  back once those were turned off.
- New: Chat (Interface > Chat), off until you switch it on. Two chat
  panels you place in Edit Mode, with sizes, backgrounds and a tab strip;
  the main chat window sits in the left one. Blizzard's chat borders,
  buttons and scroll bar are cleared; chat and tab fonts, the selected
  tab's colour, where the edit box sits, fading, lines kept, jumping back
  to the newest line and timestamps (your local time). It stays off while
  ElvUI's chat is on; your ElvUI chat settings are copied over the first
  time. More of ElvUI's chat options follow.
- Squizzumables now ships CallbackHandler-1.0 itself. Its fonts list had
  been relying on another addon to load it.

V2.7

- New: four more bundled sounds, "Defensives", "Herding Cats",
  "Herding Cats 2" and "Kelli Flailing".

V2.6

- New: two more bundled sounds, "Nori" and "SQUIZZ! From Upsidedownland".

V2.5

- New (WoW Forever): Hunter aspects are class buffs. While no aspect is up,
  each one you know gets a button: Hawk, Monkey, Wild, Beast, Cheetah and
  Pack. Untick any you don't want on the Class Buffs tab.
- Fixed (WoW Forever): the No Pet reminder no longer shows before you have
  learned Call Pet at level 10.
- Fixed: the lust song could replay after a /reload or a loading screen
  (porting to a city) while you still had Sated or Exhaustion.

V2.4

- New: three bundled sounds, "The Ducky of the Opera", "For Ducky, Every
  Song Is Sung" and "He Started It".

V2.3

- New: Squizzumables now runs on WoW Forever as well as retail. Forever has
  its own food, flask, weapon oil and class buff lists, with every rank of a
  spell counted. Buttons cast your highest rank and stay hidden until you
  have learned the spell. Rogue poisons appear with the weapon oils.
- Not on Forever yet: elixirs, Paladin blessings and augment runes.

V2.2

- New: Rotation Helper (Cooldowns > Rotation Helper, off until you switch
  it on). Shows the spell Blizzard's rotation helper suggests next on an
  icon you can put anywhere with Edit Mode, with the cooldown swipe, red
  when out of range, grey when it can't be cast (blue when you're short on
  resources) and a glow on procs. It is Blizzard's suggestion - one spell
  at a time - shown where you want it.
- New: a Ready Now row beside the Rotation Helper icon (its own tab on the
  same page). List the spells you want, per spec and in your order, or
  fill it from the rotation helper's spells that have cooldowns. Each icon
  is bright when the spell is ready and fades while it is on cooldown
  (how far is up to you, down to hidden), with the same swipe, range,
  resource and proc cues as the main icon.
- The hunter NO PET reminder now also shows when your pet is DEAD (as
  PET DEAD, so you know to Revive it rather than Call it), including in the
  middle of a fight - before, a dead pet still counted as "having a pet".
- Fixed: text reminders (NO PET, NO FOOD IN BAGS, BEACON and the rest) drew
  on top of Blizzard's windows, such as the world map and the Great Vault.
  They now sit behind them.
- Rotation Helper icons show their keybind (from your action bars, the same
  as the Cooldown Manager groups; size, position and colour on the Main
  Icon tab), and can show cooldown numbers.
- New: Low Health Glow for Cooldown Manager and custom groups (Behaviour
  tab). Tick the icons in a group that should glow while your health is
  below a threshold you set (default 35%) and the ability is ready, in a
  colour of your own - a nudge to use a defensive or a potion. Works in
  combat.
- Fixed: potions and Healthstones you added to Blizzard's Cooldown Manager
  showed on Blizzard's bars but never on ours. Combat Potion, Health
  Potion, Healthstone and Demonic Healthstone now show with their
  cooldowns, read from whichever one you carry, with that item's own icon.
  An icon only shows while you have one in your bags.
