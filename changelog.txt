Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.0

- Cooldown Alerts: the CDM Sounds page has moved to Kelerts, beside Buff
  Alerts, and cooldown alerts can now show an image as well as play a
  sound - the same images as Buff Alerts (screen edge glow, flames, the
  ( ) arcs and the rest, or your own). Pick how long it stays: a few
  seconds, or until the spell's next event (a Ready image stays up until
  you use the spell). It works in combat. Click an alert in the list to
  set its image; an alert can be image-only.
- Buffs are set up in Buff Alerts only now. Cooldown Alerts lists your
  Essential and Utility cooldowns, with Available and On Cooldown. Any
  buff sounds you had on the old CDM Sounds page (Active, On Aura Applied,
  On Aura Removed) are moved to Buff Alerts automatically, without
  replacing a sound you had already set there.
- /squizz cdms now opens Cooldown Alerts.
- Fixed: every reminder button could appear at once, out of combat, when
  the game had stopped letting addons see your buffs (seen in the 12.1.5
  labyrinth after a /reload; only logging out cleared it). The buttons now
  keep showing what they last knew until your buffs can be read again,
  the same as they already did in combat.
