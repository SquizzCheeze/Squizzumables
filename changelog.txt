Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.77

- Fixed the Cooldown Manager disappearing mid-fight whenever someone joined or
  left the group. The game reports a group member's specialisation with the
  same event it uses for your own, and the Cooldown Manager was treating every
  one of those as you changing spec: it cleared its groups to rebuild them, and
  combat does not allow the rebuild, so they stayed gone until the fight ended.
  It now only reacts to your own spec, and never clears anything during combat
