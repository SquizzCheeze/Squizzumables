Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.97

- Fixed: two different buffs with the same name (Howl of the Pack Leader
  has two) were merged into one icon in Buff Alerts, so an image could not
  be given to just one of them - it showed for both. They are now listed
  separately, an image set while they were merged is untangled, and
  Blizzard's spell alert art is only hidden for the one with the image.
- Buff Alerts: "Also show for" lets one icon answer for other buffs with
  the same name. Blizzard lists some buffs as several separate entries -
  Howl of the Pack Leader's Wyvern, Boar and Bear are three, alongside a
  fourth, unrelated Howl - so tick the ones that belong together and they
  share one image and one set of sounds, and leave the list.
- Fixed: buff images placed round your character (the ( ) arcs and the
  other shapes) were drawn on top of the spellbook, the world map and other
  windows. They now sit behind your interface, like the full-screen images.
