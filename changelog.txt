Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.76

- Unlock mode now previews every Cooldown Manager group, so you can see how
  the icons will sit while you position them. Groups set to show only in
  combat, only in instances, only with a target, or hidden while mounted used
  to be an invisible box in unlock mode; they now appear in full. Hide Until
  Active groups show every icon, and the Buffs and Buff Bars groups fill in a
  sample for each tracked buff that is not currently up. Each group's label
  also says when it will really be shown, e.g. "Essential (combat only)"
- Always Show Buffs on the Buff Bars group now draws its placeholders as bars,
  with the spell's icon and name, instead of stretching the icon across the
  whole bar
- New Cooldown Manager icon shapes: Diamond, Hexagon, Shield, Heart and Star,
  alongside Square and Round. The cooldown sweep and the border follow the
  shape, and border thickness and colour work as before
- Round icons now have a round cooldown sweep and a round border. Both used to
  stay square around the circle
- On shaped icons, the proc glow and Glow When Ready now follow the shape too:
  the game's own glow animation, burst and all, redrawn in each shape and in
  your glow colour. Square icons keep the game's glow exactly as it was
- Cooldown Manager glows now draw above neighbouring frames. A group sitting
  right next to another addon's bar could have the edge of its glow cut off
  underneath that bar's border
- Tracked buffs and buff bars are now drawn by this addon instead of borrowed
  from Blizzard's Cooldown Manager, so each buff group's border, zoom, icon
  shape, background, stack and timer text settings finally apply to them. The
  duration sweep, stacks and timer keep working in combat, because the game
  fills them in itself. Buff bars get their own Bar Colour. Untick "Draw Buffs
  Ourselves" on the Cooldown Manager page to go back to Blizzard's frames.
  Totem-style entries that are not real auras still use Blizzard's
- The Cooldowns settings page is split into sub-tabs: General for the module
  switches, then one each for Essential, Utility, Buffs and Buff Bars, instead
  of every group's settings in one long scroll. Search takes you to the right
  sub-tab
