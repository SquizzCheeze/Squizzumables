Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V2.1

- Fixed: a group buff button (Skyfury, Arcane Intellect, Battle Shout and
  the rest) could get stuck on screen after you had the buff, together with
  an "Unknown macro option: buff" error, until a /reload. The button was
  trying to hide itself in combat with a rule the game does not support. It
  now just goes away on the next refresh out of combat.
- Class buff buttons now show during a Mythic+ key, out of combat:
  Arcane Intellect, Skyfury, Earth Shield, Water and Lightning Shield,
  poisons, paladin auras and Rites, and the rest. They used to be hidden
  for the whole key. Inside a key the group buffs hide in combat like the
  other buttons, where elsewhere they stay up so you can rebuff someone.
- The options window is now the same window as SquizzFrames' (shared
  between the two addons). It looks and works as before - the same pages,
  search, Unlock Frames button and previews - with each page's title and
  description along the top. The Close button at the bottom is gone; use
  the X or Escape. Search no longer lists each setting twice.
- /squizz now opens one window for Squizzumables and SquizzFrames when
  both are installed (on its own, it opens Squizzumables as before): the
  other addon is listed at the bottom of the sidebar, search covers both,
  and picking one of its pages or settings switches to it in the same
  spot. /sq config opens Squizzumables on its own, and /squizz cdms is now
  also /sq cdms.
- The Frames Unlocked box (Lock All / Done) no longer shows in Blizzard's
  Edit Mode, which has its own Save and close. It still shows with
  /sq unlock and the options window's Unlock Frames.
- Fixed: with Hide Blizzard's Cooldown Manager on, Blizzard's own cooldown
  bars and icons sat in the top-left corner of the screen in Blizzard's
  Edit Mode. They are now moved fully off screen while it is open.
- Fixed: in Blizzard's Edit Mode the Buffs and Buff Bars groups showed as
  empty boxes. They now show the same styled sample icons and bars as
  Unlock Frames.
- Fixed: pet buttons (Call Pet, Summon Imp and the rest) stopped showing
  inside a Mythic+ key in 2.0, so after the key's start dismissed your pet
  there was no button to call it back. The buttons stopped updating
  whenever the game hid aura information, which it can do for a whole key;
  the pet buttons need none and now always update out of combat.
- Hunters: a Revive Pet button now appears, out of combat, when your pet
  has died. Before, nothing showed: the Call Pet buttons only appear with
  no pet at all, and a dead pet still counts as one.
- The theme is now shared with SquizzFrames: picking a preset in either
  addon changes both. It is saved for your whole account rather than per
  profile, so switching profiles no longer changes it. Your current choice
  carries over.
