Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.99

- Fixed: buff sounds could all stop until you logged out, with a
  "Squizzumables tried to call the protected function" error, if a rebuild
  of them happened during a Mythic+ key, a boss encounter or a PvP match.
  Those count as restricted even out of combat, and only combat was being
  waited out. The rebuild now waits for the key, boss or match to finish,
  and the sounds you already had keep playing in the meantime.
