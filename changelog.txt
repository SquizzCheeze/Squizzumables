Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.89

- Your own sounds now have their own folder:
  Interface\AddOns\SquizzumablesMedia\Sounds\ (make the folders if they are
  not there). They used to go inside Squizzumables' own Media\Sounds folder,
  which an addon update replaces - so every update quietly deleted them.
  Sounds you already added keep working and are marked "(old folder)" in the
  list; move the file across and add it again before your next update.

- Adding a custom sound is friendlier. Type the filename - with or without
  .ogg/.mp3 - press Test to hear it, then Add. The name fills itself in from
  the file unless you type one. Every outcome now says something: a file that
  cannot be found, a name you already use, and game sound being switched off
  all get a message instead of the old silent nothing.

- New bundled sound: Chemical X.

- MP3 files work. They were blocked by the addon itself, which stuck ".ogg"
  on the end of any other filename.

- Custom sounds each have a play button in the list, like the bundled ones.

- The list of your sounds now sits below the add form instead of drawing
  over it.

- The CDM settings now show a live preview of each group, drawn at the same
  size as on your screen: pinned above the settings on the Essential,
  Utility, Buffs and Buff Bars tabs, and under each group on Custom Icons.
  It is drawn by the same code as the real groups, so every setting -
  size, spacing, rows, growth, shape, border, zoom, text, colours - shows up
  in it as you change it. What only happens in play is shown as an example:
  one icon glowing, one on cooldown, one with stacks, and buffs as they look
  while up. An empty group shows stand-in icons, and a group too big for the
  pane is scaled down to fit and says so.

- The "Cooldowns" tab is now called "CDM".

- The CDM tab's groups no longer all say "0 assigned". They fill themselves
  from Blizzard's Cooldown Manager, so the count looked at a list that is
  always empty; they now show how many they actually hold.
