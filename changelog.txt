Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.85

- Tracked buffs drawn on Blizzard's own frames now take your group's icon
  shape, zoom and swipe, instead of staying square while the icons beside them
  were round.

  A buff group draws two kinds of icon: this addon's own, and Blizzard's
  frames borrowed in place for anything it cannot draw itself. Only the first
  kind was ever styled, so a row could come out half round and half square
  with nothing in the settings to explain which was which. Whatever is in the
  slot is styled now -- Blizzard's frame, a placeholder, or one of ours.

  Three things had to change for that, and each was invisible on its own.
  Blizzard masks its own icons with a rounded square, and masks combine rather
  than replace, so a circle laid over it came out as the overlap. Its icons
  are drawn at full size, so the square art's corners showed through a round
  cut. And each icon carries a square border drawn OUTSIDE the frame that no
  mask can reach at all

- Icon Zoom now does something on the buff groups. It only ever reached this
  addon's own icons, so on a group showing Blizzard's frames the slider moved
  and nothing happened

- Fixed a tracked buff disappearing entirely instead of being drawn.

  A buff whose name matches one of your cooldowns -- Bone Shield on a Death
  Knight, which is both -- could end up expected to use one of this addon's
  icons while no such icon existed for it, and the slot was then dropped in
  silence. Blizzard was still drawing it perfectly the whole time. Blizzard's
  own frame is now the floor: if there is nothing of ours to put in a slot,
  the game's own frame goes there rather than nothing at all

- Buff groups no longer depend on this addon having recognised a buff before
  they will show Blizzard's. A group that had never been handed one of our
  entries never looked at the game's own list at all, so its buffs were
  invisible while the game was plainly showing them
