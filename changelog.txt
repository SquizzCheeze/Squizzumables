Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.84

- Reminder buttons now clear the moment you apply something. Poisons, oils,
  flasks and food could sit there for several seconds after you had already
  used them, which read as the addon simply not noticing.

  Rogue poisons and weapon oils are temporary weapon enchants, and nothing was
  telling this addon they had been applied. An enchant puts no buff on you, so
  the buff event never fires; and once applied it consumes nothing from your
  bags, so the bag event never fires either. The reminder only cleared when
  some unrelated event happened along -- in a group that was usually somebody
  else's buffs changing, which is why it felt quick in a raid and slow on your
  own.

  It now watches for the enchant itself, and also refreshes on your own casts,
  so a poison clears its reminder as the cast lands rather than whenever the
  next thing happens to occur
