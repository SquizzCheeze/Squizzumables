Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.90

- Fixed "Show While Active" on Essential/Utility icons showing the ability's
  cooldown instead of its duration for some abilities, such as Sentinel and
  the Vile Vial of Volatile Venom, while the active glow still worked. If the
  buff was already up when the icon's active display was being set up, the
  setup failed and was thrown away. Seen with Sentinel and the Vile Vial; any
  ability could hit it. Active icons (and natively drawn Tracked Buffs) now
  draw their border in a way that cannot fail like that.

- Show While Active now also recognises the buff when a talent has replaced
  the spell, and learns the buff a random-effect trinket actually gave you,
  from what Blizzard's own Cooldown Manager matched. Each one is learned the
  first time it is seen out of combat, and from then on it works in combat
  too.

- Better performance in combat and in groups:
  - The Cooldown Manager did its full update several times over for a single
    aura change or cast: once for the event, then again for each tracked buff
    Blizzard updated. It now does it once per frame, however many things ask.
  - The reminder buttons were rebuilt whenever ANY unit's auras changed,
    enemy nameplates included, so fighting a pack rebuilt them up to five
    times a second. They now only react to you, your pet and your group.
  - Tracked-aura refreshes are spread out instead of all running at once.
  - Unlock mode no longer creates three new preview frames every time the
    buttons update; the same three are reused.

- Every slider's value can now be typed. Click the number at the top right of
  a slider, type an exact value and press Enter. It is kept within the
  slider's range; Escape or clicking away puts the old value back. Sliders
  with fractional steps now show their decimals instead of rounding down.

- Cooldown groups can anchor to other addons' frames, not just to each other.
  Anchor To now lists SquizzFrames' cast bars, unit frames, resource bar and
  party frames - for example, Utility under the player cast bar. Any other
  addon's frame works too: type its name into the new "Or anchor to a frame by
  name" box (/fstack shows names) and press Enter. If that frame is itself
  attached to the group, the anchor is ignored instead of breaking the layout.

- Other addons can now list your cooldown groups, custom ones included
  (Squizzumables_GetCDMGroupNames). SquizzFrames uses it so your own groups
  appear in its "Attach To" lists.
