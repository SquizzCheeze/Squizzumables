Squizzumables Changelog
=======================

V1.98

- Shark Bite (Cursor > Trail, under Click Burst; off by default): when you left-click, a
  pixel-art shark surfaces from the water under your cursor, chomps over
  it and sinks back with a splash. Purely for fun - your cursor and your
  click work as normal. Choose how often it happens (a chance per click),
  its size, and whether it only bites on clicks in the game world rather
  than on windows and buttons. A Test button shows it straight away.
- Sharks, a new cursor Trail Style: little shark silhouettes that face the
  way you move the mouse, like the ducks.
